Measured 2026-09-01 across 16 control-center-pro sessions: every reviewer ever spawned — 40 of them — returned ~15k tokens to the main thread in total, against ~15M spent by a single working session. A typical three-commit review packet is 42KB, read once per reviewer and discarded, and reviewer-taste runs on a cheap model. When someone tries to economise by cutting the standing review pass, this is the number that says they are cutting the wrong thing: the expensive habit is reading the repo into the main window, not spawning agents.
